### What was found
- **Bug (fixed): removing all styles on ChatGPT sent the message.** It sent the text as it was before the edit, `;;` included.
  - Cause: ProseMirror reads DOM edits made in the same tick as one change. "Delete `;;` in paragraph 1 + empty paragraph 3" spans blocks and matches its "this was an Enter" rule. ChatGPT's Enter handler then sends with the stale state.
  - Add and replace did not trigger it, because they end by inserting text.
  - The first fix attempt (keep the paragraph, empty only its text) was not enough; splitting the edits across ticks fixed it.
- **Two real chats were created** in the user's ChatGPT account during this test: "Compare Two Laptops" and "比较两款笔记本". A third send was blocked by the test guard and left no chat.
- Everything else worked as designed:
  - Manager (localhost demo):
    - Library: new, edit, reset and delete for styles and prompts.
    - Send: `;;` tray, prompt insert, style ticks update the Answer button and preview, Esc removes `;;`, the ✎ opens Library → Styles → Table.
    - Projects: Answer tray on Combine updates the message line in place; the tray survives the 2 s poll.
  - ChatGPT (ProseMirror) and Gemini (Quill, dark page): overlay opens; add, replace and remove of the styles line with no duplicates and no sends (with guards); prompt insert; memory insert with the `[Project memory v0: …]` label; Esc then typing does not reopen.
